What We Do
We buy, hold, and operate commercial and industrial real estate.
Flogal Properties is the real estate arm of Flogal Holdings. We focus specifically on commercial and industrial assets — properties that work for a living. Truck yards, flex industrial, warehouses, laydown yards, and terminals in freight corridors and growth markets. We are direct buyers, not intermediaries.
