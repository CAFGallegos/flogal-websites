#### 2. How we obtain your consent

We obtain your consent before sending any text message, in one of two ways. **Text-message consent is optional. It is not required to submit an inquiry, to receive a quote, or to do business with us.**

**Verbally**, during a phone call or in person. A Flogal representative asks:

> "May we text you at the number you provided regarding your inquiry or ongoing business conversation? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out."

You must affirmatively agree before any text is sent.

**Through a form**, where you check the optional text-message box next to your phone number. The box is never pre-checked. Leaving it unchecked has no effect on your inquiry.

We record the date and time of consent, the number, the method, and the representative or form involved.

#### 6. How to opt out

You may withdraw your consent at any time, by any reasonable means. Replying **STOP**, **QUIT**, **END**, **CANCEL**, **UNSUBSCRIBE**, **REVOKE**, or **OPT OUT** to any message will stop messages to that number. You may also withdraw consent by telling any Flogal representative by phone or in person, or by emailing privacy@flogalhq.com. You do not have to use any particular word or method.

We will honor your request within a reasonable time, not to exceed 10 business days. You will receive one confirmation message. After that, Flogal and its affiliated companies will not send further text messages to that number unless you give consent again.

Opting out of text messages does not end your business relationship with us. We will contact you by phone or email instead.
