FAQ

Common questions.

What is Flogal Carriers?

Flogal Carriers is the asset-based hauling division of Flogal, operating through two owned fleets: RRTL and J&D Hauling.

Is Flogal Carriers a broker?

No. Freight moves on trucks Flogal owns and dispatches, or through vetted partner carriers we work with directly.

Where does Flogal Carriers operate?

Primarily the Oklahoma–Texas corridor, with partner reach across the U.S. and cross-border into Mexico through trusted Mexico partners.

What does RRTL haul?

End-dump aggregate: rock, sand, and base material out of regional quarries.
