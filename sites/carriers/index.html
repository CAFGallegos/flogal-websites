How we operate

From RFP to POD on a single accountability chain.

One operator, one ops desk, four steps. The whole load lives on a single transaction — no second-tier brokers, no double-dispatched confusion, no calls about who actually has your trailer.

01

Quote

Share lane, equipment, weight, and pickup window — our ops desk quotes it directly, no broker markup.

02

Dispatch

We assign owned equipment or a vetted partner fleet and you get the driver and truck before pickup.

03

FAQ

Common questions.

What is Flogal Carriers?

Flogal Carriers is the asset-based hauling division of Flogal, operating through fleets we run.

Is Flogal Carriers a broker?

No. Freight moves on trucks Flogal owns and dispatches, or through vetted partner carriers we work with directly.

Where does Flogal Carriers operate?

Primarily the Oklahoma–Texas corridor, with partner reach across the U.S. and cross-border into Mexico through trusted Mexico partners.

What does the fleet haul?

End-dump aggregate: rock, sand, and base material out of regional quarries.
