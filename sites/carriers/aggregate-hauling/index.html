FAQ

Common questions.

Who does aggregate hauling in Oklahoma?

Flogal Carriers hauls aggregate across Oklahoma and Texas through its owned fleet RRTL, running end-dump and belly-dump trailers out of regional quarries.

What is end dump hauling?

End dump hauling moves loose bulk material — rock, sand, gravel — in trailers that unload by raising the bed. It's the standard method for quarry and jobsite aggregate delivery.

Do you handle recurring quarry runs?

Yes. Daily quarry freight is the core of the operation — recurring lanes are what our dispatch desk is built around.
