Common questions.

What does Flogal Sales sell?

Flogal Sales sells and leases used Class 8 tractors (day cab and sleeper), dry van, reefer, and flatbed trailers, and heavy equipment.

Can I see a unit in person?

Yes. Units are staged at working Flogal yards and shown by appointment.

Does Flogal Sales offer leasing?

Yes. Most units are available for purchase or lease, including lease-to-own on select equipment.

Can Flogal source specific equipment?

Yes. The Flogal equipment desk sources trucks, trailers, and machinery to spec through auctions, OEM allocations, and lease-end returns.
