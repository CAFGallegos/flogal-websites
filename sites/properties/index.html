FAQ

Common questions.

What is Flogal Properties?

Flogal Properties is the industrial real estate arm of Flogal, acquiring freight-oriented property — yards, terminals, and truck-ready sites — on active freight corridors.

Does Flogal Properties buy property directly?

Yes. Flogal Properties is an operator-funded direct buyer of freight-oriented industrial real estate, including off-market sites.

What kind of property does Flogal Properties look for?

Drop yards, terminals, truck parking, and industrial land that supports freight operations, primarily in Oklahoma and Texas freight corridors.

Sell us your site.

Own a yard, terminal, or industrial parcel on a freight corridor? We're a direct buyer — operator-funded, no committee, structured to close. Off-market conversations welcome.
