Flogal Technologies

Run like one company, not five.

Every Flogal company runs on the same internal operating system — Flogal Technologies. Maintenance, driver tickets, payroll, and per-truck profitability live in one platform we built and operate ourselves. That means every truck's real cost is visible, every load is auditable, and a new company can plug into working systems on day one — not rebuild them. It's not a product we sell; it's how we keep freight companies disciplined, auditable, and ready to scale.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is Flogal?

Flogal is an operator-funded freight platform. It builds and operates a group of freight-native companies — hauling, equipment sales and leasing, and industrial real estate — on a shared operating system.

Is Flogal a carrier or a broker?

Flogal operates asset-based carriers through J&D Hauling and RRTL. It is not a freight broker.

Where does Flogal operate?

Flogal's freight operations run primarily on the Oklahoma–Texas corridor. The team also brings nationwide over-the-road and US–Mexico cross-border freight experience.

What is Flogal Technologies?

Flogal Technologies is Flogal's internal operating system for maintenance, dispatch, payroll, and per-truck P&L. It runs Flogal's own companies; it is not sold as software.

Does Flogal acquire companies?

Yes. Flogal partners with and acquires freight businesses, fleets, and freight-oriented assets that fit its platform. Sellers and operators can reach out through the contact form.
